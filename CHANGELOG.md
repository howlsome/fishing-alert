# Changelog

## v0.5.0

First public release.

- Big pulsing marker above the bobber: fishing-hook icon, border and arrow, with size and colour settings.
- Option to hide the "Fishing Bobber" text above the bobber.
- Screen-edge flashes when the fish got away, the line ran out, or the cast missed the water.
- One key (F or Space) to cast and reel in while a fishing pole is equipped.
- Auto-apply your best lure, and a lure picker on Shift + fishing key.
- Gamepad support: pad fishing button, pad lure picker button, D-pad picker controls. Shown only when a controller is connected.
- Settings page under Options > AddOns, opened with `/fa`.
- Optional countdown bar (off by default).
